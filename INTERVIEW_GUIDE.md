# Interview Guide — The Group Coordinator

**Length:** 15–20 min · **Format:** casual chat, in person or call · **Record?** Ask first; otherwise take quick notes.

## Who to talk to
- **2 coordinators:** people who usually plan trips for groups of 4–8 friends.
- **1 group member who doesn't plan:** they'll show you the other side, like what makes people go quiet, drag their feet or drop out.

## Ground rules for you
- Ask about **the last real trip**, not trips in general. Real stories beat opinions.
- Don't mention AI until the end. You want to hear their actual behaviour, not ideas for features.
- When something interesting comes up, follow with "Tell me more", "Why?" and "What happened next?"

---

## 1. Warm-up (2 min)
- Tell me about the last trip you planned with a group. Where, how many people, how did it go?
- Did it actually happen as first planned?

## 2. Coordination (5 min)
- How did the idea start? Who said it first, and where (a WhatsApp group, in person)?
- How did the group agree on **dates**? On **budget**? On **where to go**?
- Did you use polls, spreadsheets, shared docs, or just chat?
- Where did things **get stuck**? How long did it stay stuck?
- What finally made everyone commit?
- What was the most annoying part of being the one in charge?

## 3. How people behave (5 min)
First, **without showing any list:**
- Describe the people in that group. Who was easy? Who was hard? Why?
- Was there someone who said "I'm fine with anything" and then wasn't?

**Then show this card** and ask: *"Which of these were in your group? How did you handle them?"*

| Type | Typical behaviour |
|---|---|
| The Ghost | Goes silent in the group chat and only replies once things are decided |
| The Budget Worrier | Tight on money but won't say a number out loud |
| The Vetoer | Rejects options without suggesting a better one |
| The "Anything works" | Agrees to everything, complains later |
| The Late Dropper | Commits, then cancels close to the date |
| The Researcher | Sends 20 links and reviews, slows down the decision |
| The Deal Hunter | Keeps checking for cheaper prices after booking |

- Who's missing from this list?
- Which type causes the most trouble for the planner?

## 4. Who does what (4 min)
- Who booked what (flights, stays, activities)? How was that decided?
- Did roles get **assigned**, did people **volunteer**, or did it all **fall to you** by default?
- Who paid upfront? How did money get split and chased afterwards?
- Did anyone feel left out of decisions, or have too much say?
- If you could hand off one job to someone else, which one?

## 5. Trust and handing over control (2 min)
*Only now bring up AI.*
- Imagine an assistant that helps plan group trips. What would you be happy to let it **decide for you**?
- What would you **never** let it decide without asking?
- What would it need to show you before you'd trust its suggestion?

## 6. Close (1 min)
- Is there anything about planning group trips I should have asked but didn't?
- Thank them.
